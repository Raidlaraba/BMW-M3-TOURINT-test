The First BMW M3 Wagon

The M3 Touring (G81) launched in 2022 - the first-ever production M3 wagon in the nameplate's history. BMW had M5 touring wgons before (E34,E60) but never an M3 wagon until 2022 - this is a genuinely new addition M history. Combines M3 Competition performance with wagon practicality - you get the S58 twin-turbo engine and M xDrive AWD, plus a proper cargo hold (500-1510L)

April Fool's joke

April 2025, BMW M Motorsport posted an April Fool'joke on social media showing a concept render of an M3 Touring race car - originally meant puely as a gag

Reaction

The post went massively viral (1,6M+ views), and funs pushed for BMW to actually build it

Summer 2025

BMW greenlit it as a real project

Manufacturing

8-Month devlopment - Built on the M4 gt3 EVO platform (same base as the coupe GT3 race car), but with a body 200mm longer and 32mm taller to fit the wagon shape - everything else mechanically matches the coupe
